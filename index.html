    Who decides what the next knowledge infrastructure remembers?

    Let Taiwan use its own sourcesto understand its stories.

    General-purpose models optimize for average performance across markets. They have little incentive to preserve each place’s historical context, minority languages, and cultural memory. Its sources also show how democratic institutions, culture, and everyday life shape public memory.

    Public knowledge has institutional, cultural, and everyday dimensions. Each asks a different question of the sources.

    Knowledge has more than one layer.

    A useful answer can be checked for facts while leaving room for differences in language, values, and interpretation.

    01

    Factual knowledge

    Dates, names, laws, and reporting that can be checked against a source.

    02

    Language in use

    Local terms and expressions, including Indigenous languages, Taiwanese, and Hakka, whose meaning depends on context.

    03

    Explicit social norms

    Legal reasoning, rights, and civic debate that frame how a claim is understood.

    04

    Narrative, interpretation & framing

    Different accounts of the same event, preserved with their sources so readers can examine the distance between them.

    Common questions

    FAQ

    About dates, verification, and rights.

    What periods does NewsLLM cover?+

    The interactive timeline begins in 1624 with Taiwan’s history and research leads. The earliest public media news material dates to 1972; date ranges vary by publisher.

    Can I verify a report?+

    Reports are designed to show the publisher, date, and original link. Display of supporting passages depends on source permissions. Check important conclusions against the original text and its context.

    Why preserve differing accounts?+

    The same event may appear differently in news, court records, legislative proceedings, and community memory. Keeping those differences visible helps readers understand a conclusion’s basis and limits.

    Can I republish full articles?+

    Inclusion in the collection does not grant rights to republish full articles. Follow the original publisher’s terms for reading and reuse.
